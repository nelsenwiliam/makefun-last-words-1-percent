Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
**Famous Last Words at 1 Percent** ($2): the funniest possible last words
a phone would say when its battery hits 1 percent, as a single line of at
most 199 characters.

## The line

> One percent left. I could save your unsent text, warn you about your
> meeting, or call your mother. Instead, I'm dying dramatically, in
> silence, like an artist.

Ten candidate lines were written and scored against the judge's brief
(on subject, bigger laugh with a real twist, the phone's own voice, a
tight line with the punch at the end, fresh — no will reading, no
goodbye to the charger, no pun on 1 percent). This one won: three noble
acts set up the twist, and "like an artist" is the sharp final beat.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes entry.txt (or /output/entry.txt in the harness)
```

Pure Python standard library, no third-party packages.
